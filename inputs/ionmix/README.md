# Real-material IONMIX EOS + opacity tables (Al / Be / D)

Developer-supplied **FLASH IONMIX `.cn4`** equation-of-state and multigroup-opacity tables
for the three MagLIF benchmark materials (issue [C1]/#118, ADR-0007). Each file is a
*combined* table carrying both the natively-3T EOS records (mean ionization, separate
electron/ion specific energies, pressures and heat capacities) and the multigroup opacity
records (Rosseland transport, Planck absorption, Planck emission).

| File | Material | Z | ntemp × ndens | groups | T range [eV] | n_ion range [cm⁻³] |
|------|----------|---|---------------|--------|--------------|--------------------|
| `al-imx-004.cn4` | aluminum   | 13 | 21 × 25 | 30 | 1 – 1.0e5   | 1e16 – 1e24 |
| `Be-006-imx.cn4` | beryllium  | 4  | 21 × 21 | 6  | 0.2 – 2.0e4 | 1e16 – 1e26 |
| `DD-006-imx.cn4` | deuterium  | 1  | 21 × 21 | 6  | 0.2 – 2.0e4 | 1e16 – 1e26 |

(`DD-006-imx.cn4` is a 50/50 D mixture, atomic numbers `1 1`, fractions `0.5 0.5`.)

## Format & provenance

- **Format:** the packed fixed-width "cn4" format FLASH reads at runtime — a 4-line header
  (`ntemp ndens`; atomic numbers; relative fractions; `ngroups`) followed by 12-character
  fixed-width fields (4 per line) in a fixed record order. This is the format produced by
  the FLASH-center [`opacplot2`](https://github.com/flash-center/opacplot2) reference
  reader/writer (`opg_ionmix.py`); the IONMIX tables themselves are generated by
  J. J. MacFarlane's IONMIX code. See `src/utils/ionmix_cn4_format.hpp` for the full record
  layout this repo decodes.
- **Units (as stored — loaded faithfully, no conversion):** temperatures in eV; the density
  axis is ion **number** density [cm⁻³] (not mass density); specific energies / pressures /
  heat capacities in the IONMIX Joules-based units; opacities are mass opacities [cm²/g];
  group boundaries in eV. Downstream code-unit normalization (and the optional
  number-density → mass-density rescale via a mean ion mass) is the consumer's concern, not
  the loader's.
- **Source:** supplied by the developer for the MagLIF benchmark ladder (Ellison FLASH
  MagLIF benchmarks, arXiv:2504.10760). The `imx`/`cn4` filename suffixes are the upstream
  IONMIX naming; the integer (`004`, `006`) is the table revision.

## Consumers

- `src/eos/ionmix_eos_reader.hpp` → `ReadIonmixCn4Eos` populates `eos_table_3t::EosTable3T`.
- `src/opacity/ionmix_opacity_reader.hpp` → `ReadIonmixCn4Opacity` populates
  `opacity::MultigroupOpacity`.
- Verified by `tst/test_suite/unit_tests/test_unit_ionmix_cn4_{eos,opacity}_cpu.py`
  (registered in `docs/verification.md`), whose oracle is the independent Python decode
  `ionmix_cn4_decode.py`.
